FAQ

How does Buy 2 Get 1 FREE work?

Add 2 eligible Basic License beats to your cart in the BeatStars store and the third beat is free. The discount should apply automatically at checkout — if it doesn't, contact me directly.

How does Buy 3 Get 2 FREE work?

Add 3 eligible Basic License beats to your cart and get 2 free. The discount should apply automatically at checkout — if it doesn't, contact me directly.

How fast is delivery?

Delivery is instant after successful payment through the store checkout.

What files do I get?

You get the high-quality files for that license by email right after payment. The file type is the one named in your BeatStars contract.

How do exclusive purchases work?

Send an offer with the beat name and your budget. After it is agreed and paid, the beat comes off public sale for the contract term. You keep the distributor master royalties. The producer still owns the beat and is paid on the publishing split in the contract.
